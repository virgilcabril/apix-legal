Delete Your APIX Load Account

Last updated: May 27, 2026

You can request deletion of your APIX Load account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how to make the request, what we delete, and what we are required by Philippine law to keep.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In the app

  1. Open APIX Load
  2. Go to Profile (bottom-right)
  3. Scroll to Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm with your PIN

Option 2 — By email

Send a message to info@apix.services with:

  • Subject: Delete my account
  • Body: the Philippine mobile number associated with your account

We acknowledge your request within 7 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Within 30 days of your request, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Saved beneficiary phone numbers
  • Linked devices and their session tokens
  • Facebook account pairing (if any)
  • PIN and password records
  • Crash logs associated with your account

What we are required to keep

By Philippine law we must retain certain records even after account deletion:

  • Wallet transaction records — 5 years. Required by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) and the Anti-Money Laundering Act (AMLA) for electronic money operators.
  • Compliance audit trail — 1 year. Required for regulatory record-keeping.

These records are kept in a restricted-access archive, are not used for any other purpose, and are permanently destroyed at the end of the retention period.
